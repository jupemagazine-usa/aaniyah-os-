Welcome to the session

A note for whoever found their way here.

Thank you for stopping by. This little desktop is a front porch for the music — a quiet place to listen, to look around, and to stay a while.

Everything here was made with care in New Orleans: songs written to be felt, films made to be watched slowly, and a label built to carry them — Sweet Vanilla Records.

Press play on something. Leave a kind word in the guestbook if the music moved you. And come back — the session is always in session.

— Aaniyah.
